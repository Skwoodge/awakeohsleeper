# Awake Oh Sleeper Productions

Suicide awareness website for a punk/ska/reggae concert organization based in Prescott, AZ.

## Mission

"To unite people through the energy and authenticity of punk rock, ska, hardcore, and reggae music, using live concerts as a platform to spread hope, love, and connection. Raising awareness around mental health and suicide prevention, breaking stigma, and creating spaces where people feel seen, supported, and empowered to seek help."

## Design System

### Colors (CSS Custom Properties)
- `--black-deep`: #040606 (bg primary)
- `--black-mid`: #0a0c0b (card bg)
- `--green-neon`: #e9f00b (accent-primary, neon text)
- `--green-lime`: #afd734 (accent-secondary)
- `--green-medium`: #81a341 (accent-tertiary, mono text)
- `--green-forest`: #315a18 (scrollbar)
- `--cream`: #ebedc4
- `--white-warm`: #e8e8e0 (text-primary)
- `--text-secondary`: #a0a098
- `--glow-green`: rgba(233, 240, 11, 0.3)
- `--glow-green-strong`: rgba(233, 240, 11, 0.6)

### Spacing
- `--space-xs`: 0.25rem | `--space-sm`: 0.5rem | `--space-md`: 1rem
- `--space-lg`: 2rem | `--space-xl`: 4rem | `--space-2xl`: 8rem

### Key CSS Classes
- `.neon-text` — green glow text shadow (accent-primary)
- `.neon-text-sm` — lighter green glow
- `.section-label` — uppercase mono label with green accent bar
- `.btn`, `.btn-primary`, `.btn-secondary`, `.btn-ticket` — button variants
- `.btn-glow` — adds neon glow to buttons
- `.btn-arrow` — animated bouncing arrow on CTA buttons
- `.stagger-children` — children stagger in with delays when visible
- `.countdown-number.tick` — white flash animation on countdown number change
- `.hero-parallax` — parallax depth effect on scroll
- `.section`, `.section-dark`, `.section-darker` — section backgrounds
